    Build notes

    How this is built

    The rotating aerial views are rendered from a 3D model built from the architect's drawings (site plan, floor plans, sections): two six-level courtyard blocks, the commercial building, the courtyard pools, decks, pergolas and play areas, on the real site in Montijo. Only the project is modelled; the surroundings are fields and the real road network. The studio renders of the current design are in the Gallery and on the Amenities cards.

    Outlines are traced, not drawn

    Each still has a flat-colour ID image rendered from the same model: one colour per block, one per floor. tools/build4.py traces them into the outlines you hover.

    Units

    Fração, typology and area come from the architect's floor plans: 320 homes, 160 per block (ground floor and levels 1–4, 32 per level). The recuado roof level has terraces and storage, not homes. Prices are "on request" until the sales sheet is connected.
